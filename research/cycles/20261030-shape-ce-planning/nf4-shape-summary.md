# NF4 cycle8 — shape hypothesis and an exactness boundary

**Planning/proof only; L1-TILELANG-03 and gemm-09 remain conditional at their current priorities.** A later original inline-decode GEMM should not assume the down shape is preferable to gate/up. Both have the same weight count, but the gate/up geometry has a shorter reduction dimension and more output tiles. With the same declared tile, useful per-projection work and naive requested operand volume are equal; scheduling and input/output traffic differ. None of this establishes occupancy, latency, compiler resources or a winner.

Pinned native source admits a larger small-M custom range for gate/up than down. That is a source routing clue, not a benchmark of our implementation or permission to copy the kernel/heuristic. Keep original gate and up calls separate, with unchanged order, output casts and activation graph. [Pinned native routing](https://github.com/bitsandbytes-foundation/bitsandbytes/blob/08a9956b939857ae2c4fbd56753960cc370fa7cf/bitsandbytes/backends/cuda/ops.py#L634-L709), [Qwen MLP](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/93c8b7b485963a10800c91f55304db6be211c2bd/src/transformers/models/qwen2/modeling_qwen2.py#L35-L48).

An independent finite CPU witness distinguishes two operations. Reconstructing one separately rounded FP32 scale within its aligned quantization block, then broadcasting it **before each weight's FP16 rounding and dot**, preserves the tested decoded values. Moving that scale **after dot** changes the final result by one FP16 ULP even without overflow. The fixture is constructed, not a model observation.

This disproves equivalence to the currently declared exact FP16-weight-before-dot contract. It is **not** a CUDA outcome, whole-task quality failure or automatic contest disqualification. An approximate alternative would require explicit separate review; none is silently substituted. No measured instruction reduction follows from source-level scale reuse. [Pinned reconstruction contract](https://github.com/bitsandbytes-foundation/bitsandbytes/blob/08a9956b939857ae2c4fbd56753960cc370fa7cf/bitsandbytes/backends/cuda/ops.py#L904-L916).

The next decision remains bounded: first obtain an actual complete forward-decode result. Only after selection, compare native fallback, original exact decode plus unchanged vendor GEMM, and one original inline-GEMM hypothesis on the two shapes. Exact decoded weights precede the separately declared dot-reduction numerical gate. Complete-call time, required allocations and cold work matter; larger owned event totals are not the optimization objective. No new implementation or GPU job is authorized here.
