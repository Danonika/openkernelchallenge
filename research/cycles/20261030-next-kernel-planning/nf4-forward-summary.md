# NF4 cycle7 — forward decode with unchanged vendor GEMM

**Planning only; existing SOUP-NEW-003 and L1-TILELANG-03; priorities unchanged.** Trial002 measured only backward-functional decode and failed standalone useful share. It does not validate a forward extension or fused GEMM.

The lower-risk next hypothesis is to reuse the original exact decoder in the existing **bias-free forward fallback**, retaining the native outer dispatcher and vendor GEMM. Source-defined non-LoRA o/gate/up/down projections span 112 calls and 1,222,115,328 of 1,310,195,712 quantized forward weight values: **93.277% geometric volume**. Actual eligible routing, launch coverage, timing and own share are unmeasured. [Pinned Qwen attention](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/93c8b7b485963a10800c91f55304db6be211c2bd/src/transformers/models/qwen2/modeling_qwen2.py#L179-L192), [MLP](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/93c8b7b485963a10800c91f55304db6be211c2bd/src/transformers/models/qwen2/modeling_qwen2.py#L35-L48).

Preserve nested scale/code/offset semantics, separate FP32 rounding, input dtype and unchanged native fallback for unsupported inputs. Forward decode targets the activation dtype directly (initial FP16 hypothesis), unlike backward's state-dtype-then-gradient-dtype boundary. Retain ordinary dense weight materialization: this proposal does **not** remove the dense weight buffer or establish a training-peak saving. [Pinned forward/backward contracts](https://github.com/bitsandbytes-foundation/bitsandbytes/blob/08a9956b939857ae2c4fbd56753960cc370fa7cf/bitsandbytes/backends/cuda/ops.py#L904-L982), [backward cast order](https://github.com/bitsandbytes-foundation/bitsandbytes/blob/08a9956b939857ae2c4fbd56753960cc370fa7cf/bitsandbytes/autograd/_functions.py#L365-L386).

The decisive comparison is complete native fallback versus original decode plus the **same** vendor GEMM, including required allocations and all real work. Require exact finite decoded values/forward values and native gradient parity, corresponding nonfinite classes, layout, metadata and RNG behavior; kernel-only latency is insufficient.

The existing down-only inline NF4+FP16 GEMM idea remains a higher-risk alternative. It must beat both complete-path controls, not just a decode microbenchmark, while preserving its separate numerical contract. Repeated decode across matrix tiles remains a risk; no generic tuning expansion or standalone-share filler is justified.

A later selected portfolio could contain norm, backward decode, forward decode and entropy only if fresh combined evidence supports it. Distinct patch targets do not prove compatibility or performance. Useful share must be measured per actual run; the 5% share gate, 0.5% public-loss limit and 1.05 scoring floor are separate. Faster end-to-end execution is the optimization objective; artificial slowdown or redundant work is prohibited. [Pinned own-share checker](https://example.invalid/redacted-github).
